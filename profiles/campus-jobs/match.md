# Campus recruiting profile

Use this profile for campus recruiting (校招) job postings aimed at undergraduate students, especially software engineering, computer science, and artificial intelligence majors.

Typical items are full-time campus positions for graduating students. They include a company, role title, city, degree requirement, graduation cohort, responsibilities, and an application link.

Do not use this profile for experienced-hire (社招) roles, internships for current students, interview experience posts, company news, or non-technical positions.
